# Tailgunner

A browser-based fan recreation of the 1979 Vectorbeam arcade classic **Tailgunner**, built with [Paper.js](https://paperjs.org/) and [jsfxr](https://github.com/mneubrand/jsfxr).

Play it in your browser here:
**[https://goocto.itch.io/tailgunner](https://goocto.itch.io/tailgunner)**

<iframe src="https://goocto.com/tailgunner" width="640" height="480" frameborder="0"></iframe>

## About

This is a tribute to the vector arcade game that ate a lot of quarters back in the '80s. Spaceships are built by hand from a handful of vertices and edges, sound effects are synthesized on the fly, and high scores are saved to the cloud.

The whole game fits in a tiny package and runs straight in your browser — no install required.

## How to Play

The goal is simple: survive as long as you can and rack up the highest score.

- **Aim and shoot** — move the mouse to aim your guns; left-click fires.
- **Shields** — press the **right mouse button** (secondary button) or the **spacebar** to raise your shields and block incoming fire.
- **Energy** — shooting and shields drain your energy meter, so don't spam either.
- **Lives** — you start with a handful of ships; lose them all and it's game over.

Your best score is saved online so you can compete across sessions.
