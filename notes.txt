Make a formation of three identical fighters.

When we initialize them:
- create a formation of three vehicles somewhat in the middle of the screen +/-33% and calculate their positions fp1, fp2 and fp3
- a formation involves all three vehicles in close proximity such that every one pairs with at least one other and that paired one is within ~2 vehicle diameters
- project backwards in time using the motion parameters to find the start points sp1, sp2 and sp3
- project forwards in time using the motion parameters to find the end points ep1, ep2 and ep3

- use the current parameters 
- make sure that none of ep1, ep2, ep3 are within 2 vehicle diameters of the camera when they pass
- if they are then calculate deltas so that each endpoint is not within proximity. prefer a camera location in the middle of them all if possible.
- apply these deltas to sp1, sp2 and sp3, set the vehicles at those start points
- each vehicle should have its own parameters, but they will meet at the formation points fp1, fp2 and fp3
